I am an economist and Data Scientist Lead in the Supervision and Regulation Department at the Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Strathclyde (Glasgow, UK), supervised by Professor Gary Koop and Professor Stuart McIntyre.

My research interests are applied financial economics and Bayesian econometrics, with a focus on banking and financial stability, credit risk and stress testing, and tail-risk and distributional modeling using quantile and functional regression.
